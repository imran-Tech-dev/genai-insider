What Is Generative AI? A Beginner's Guide

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content such as text, images, audio, video, and code.

What is Generative AI?

Generative AI learns patterns from large datasets and uses those patterns to produce new outputs in response to a prompt. Modern generative systems can help with writing, brainstorming, coding, research, design, and many other tasks.

How does it work?

At a high level, a model is trained on large amounts of data. During use, your prompt provides instructions or context. The model then generates an output based on the patterns and relationships it learned during training.

Where is it used?

  • Writing and content creation
  • Software development and debugging
  • Image and video creation
  • Education and tutoring
  • Business productivity and customer support

Final thoughts

Generative AI is becoming a useful tool across many industries. The best approach for beginners is to learn the fundamentals, experiment with practical tasks, and verify important information before relying on an AI output.
